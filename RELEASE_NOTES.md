# Modern UI Suite 0.1.23

Party, Bag, PC and Pokédex now offer independent **Fill**, **16:9** and
**4:3** aspect ratios under **Options → Modern UI Suite → [component] →
Aspect Ratio**. Fill preserves the existing layout. Party's choice also
applies to Summary and its related pages. Widescreen Off and Faithful Ratio
retain their native-size behavior.

Wilds of Kanto's Follow message no longer causes Gen 2 to draw a second,
compact party over the modern roster. Native prompts use the menu's scale,
and decorative lines stay inside the chosen layout.

Gen 1 evolution covers the current menu surface while preserving its real
“is evolving” dialogue. Other native opaque child screens receive a full
backing, with centered artwork and aligned palette regions. Bag item-use
transitions retain their inherited surface.

Install `modern_ui_suite-0.1.23.zip` through the Mods
manager, then apply and restart. Existing preferences are preserved; Fill is
the default for the new options.

Validated with automated Modkit tests and native Red/Crystal captures on
Gen1Recomp 0.2.56, including Wilds of Kanto 2.1.9, ultrawide and portrait
layouts, retained evolution dialogue and Bag transitions. Android hardware
has not been tested directly.
